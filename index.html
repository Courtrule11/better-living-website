About Better Living

A home that helps you be your best self

“Everyone deserves better living. A quality home gives you the space to live better, rest well, and be your best self.” The Better Living promise

Our role in Washington County is simple: take homes that need care, renovate them to a standard we would want for our own family, and offer them as stable, welcoming rentals for real families. We started in Donora and we are growing town by town, including Charleroi, Monongahela, Monessen, Belle Vernon and beyond, so wherever in the county you land, the standard stays the same.

We are not just leasing a unit. We are investing in these neighborhoods for the long term: solid, bright, well maintained homes, clear terms, and a genuine welcome for Section 8 and veteran voucher holders. When your home is taken care of, everyday life gets easier, and you can put your energy into living better.

How much is the rent and deposit?

Unit 1 rents for $1,000 a month with a $1,000 security deposit. The lease term is 1 year, and the home is available now.

Do you accept vouchers?

Yes. Section 8 Housing Choice and HUD-VASH veteran vouchers are both welcome. Just mention your voucher when you call or text, and we will walk you through the next steps.

What utilities are included?

The owner covers city water, public sewer, trash, and lawn and snow care. You cover electric heat and electric cooking, in your name, so you control your own usage. There is no air conditioning.

Are pets allowed?

No, pets are not allowed in this home.
